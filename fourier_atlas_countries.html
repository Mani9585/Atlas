How the Fourier Atlas works

01 / SOURCE

Get a real country boundary

The app loads country polygons, including separate islands, from a world GeoJSON dataset.

02 / FOURIER ANALYSIS

Convert each ring into frequencies

Boundary points are evenly resampled to 512 samples per ring, then decomposed into complex Fourier coefficients ordered by frequency.

03 / RECONSTRUCT

Build the outline from epicycles

The selected number of terms is used to reconstruct the displayed outline. Increasing the count adds higher-frequency detail.

z(t) = Σ cₖ eikt   ·   cₖ = (1/N) Σ zⱼ e−i2πkj/N
A discrete Fourier transform is used for each closed boundary ring. The outline is cached between frames; the number of terms is limited by the sampled boundary resolution.
